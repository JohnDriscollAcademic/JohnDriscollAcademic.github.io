01 / RESEARCH

Numerical mathematics for scientific computing.

My research focuses on the development and analysis of numerical methods for differential equations, with an emphasis on methods that are accurate, stable, and computationally efficient.

01

Numerical PDEs

Discretization and computation for partial differential equations, including finite-element and spectral methods.

02

Time Integration

Runge–Kutta and diagonally implicit methods, stability, perturbations, and efficient time integration.

03

Scientific Computing

High-performance numerical algorithms, mixed-precision computation, and numerical linear algebra.
