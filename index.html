Research

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Computational Science and Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Dartmouth.

My research is in applied and computational mathematics, with a focus on numerical methods, partial differential equations, scientific computing, and numerical linear algebra.

My current work focuses on efficient numerical methods for differential equations.
